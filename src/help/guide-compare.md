Both live under **Tools › Compare** in the sidebar. They change nothing unless you save the findings as a note.

## Compare two documents
Open [Compare](app:compare), the **Any two documents** tab. Pick **A** and **B** by typing part of their names, or **Choose a file…** for a PDF, Word, PowerPoint or Excel file on disk. Choose **Differences**, or **What changed** from an earlier version (B) to a newer one (A), and click **Compare**. **Compare with…** on a source's ▾ in [Sources](app:sources) starts with that file as A.

## Read the findings
Each finding is **Differs**, **Only in A**, **Only in B** or **Agrees** (or **Changed**, **Added**, **Removed**), quoting both documents. One that changes a decision, number, date, owner or commitment is marked **Material**. If the two aren't about the same thing, [Compare](app:compare) stops after a quick look and says so.

## Set up the register for Doc check
Doc check reads a document against the governing one in force. On the **Doc check** tab ([Doc check](app:doccheck)), click **Start the register** to create `Me. Canonical Docs.md`, then add a row for each version of a policy or standard: key, title, version, status (canonical, draft or superseded), path and aliases. **Edit register** opens it later.

## Check a document
In [Doc check](app:doccheck), choose a source beside **Check**, or **Choose a file…**. Pick **Against the version in force** and the governing document, or **Against my earlier feedback** for a revision, and click **Check**. Each finding is a **Conflict**, **Not covered**, **Superseded term**, **Beyond scope** or **Agrees**.

## Work while it runs, then save
A check or comparison runs in the background, so you can leave the page: **Compare** in the sidebar shows a spinner, then a badge, and a message offers **Open**. **Stop** ends it at once. **Copy findings** copies them as markdown and **Save as note** saves them to the vault, listed in [Changes](app:review). See [Doc check](help:doccheck) and [Compare two documents](help:compare) for more.
