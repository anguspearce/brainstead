Graph draws pages as bubbles and links as lines. It updates as the vault changes.

## Centre the graph on a page
Click **Graph** in an open note's top bar, or type in **Find a page to centre on** and pick a page. The first time you open Graph from the sidebar, it shows the whole wiki.

**Neighbourhood** shows the centre page with what it links to and what links to it. The **Depth** slider (1 to 3, 2 to start) takes in links of links. **Whole wiki** shows every wiki page. Back and forward (`⌘[`, `⌘]`) step through the pages you centred on.

An assistant can read the same neighbourhood around a page, leaving out kinds you'd hide with the chips, and can open Graph around a page.

## Move around
- Hover a bubble to name it and dim all but its neighbours.
- Click a bubble to centre on it; double-click to open the page.
- Drag the background, or scroll with two fingers, to pan. Drag a bubble to move it.
- Pinch, or scroll with `⌘` held, to zoom. **Fit** fits the graph to the window again.

A large neighbourhood is cut to the nearest 400 pages, and the line under the graph says so.

## Read the colours
Each kind of page has the colour it has everywhere else in Brainstead. A wiki type is the colour of its type pill's dot and its slice on the Knowledge health pie; notes, sources and other wiki pages are the colour of their square (Note, Source, Wiki):

- Entities: sky blue.
- Concepts: olive green.
- Summaries: coral.
- Other wiki pages: teal, as Wiki's square.
- Sources: brown, as Source's square.
- Notes: blue, as Note's square.

The chips at the top left name them. A grey ring is a page that's linked to but not written yet. A bubble's size is how many links it has across the vault.

Click a chip to hide or show that kind; the centre page always stays.

## Use the side pane
With a centre page, the pane names it, says how many links it has and lists the pages linked to or from it. Click one to centre on it, or **Open** to open the centre page. On the whole wiki, the pane shows the number of pages and links, and the most linked pages.
