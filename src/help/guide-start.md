## Choose your vault
Your vault is the folder of markdown notes you already keep. On first run on a Mac Brainstead asks for Full Disk Access, then for the folder (Windows asks only for the folder); you can change it later in [Settings › Vault](app:settings/vault) with **Choose…**. Brainstead indexes the folder into its own database, outside the vault, and keeps the index current as files change in other apps.

No vault yet? Click **Create a new vault** on the first screen, give it a name and choose where to put it. It's made with a few example notes: open **Start here** for what each one shows, and delete them when you're done. To get them back, use **Add the example notes** in [Settings › Vault](app:settings/vault).

## Decide when Brainstead may write
A vault you choose opens read-only: Brainstead reads everything and changes nothing. A new vault Brainstead made opens with Read-only off, as nothing in it is yours yet. When you want to tick tasks, capture, save notes and let the daily and weekly summaries write, turn **Read-only** off in [Settings › Vault](app:settings/vault). Even then it only saves a file that hasn't changed since it read it, and every AI change is listed in Changes, where you can revert it.

## Find your way round the sidebar
The top of the sidebar is the daily flow: [Today](app:today), Inbox, Tasks, Projects, [Weekly review](app:weekly), Changes and Ask. **Library** holds Search, Notes, Wiki, Sources, Templates and Graph; **Tools** holds [Draft reply](app:reply), [Meeting notes](app:meeting) and **Compare** ([Doc check](app:doccheck) and [any two documents](app:compare)) (a spinner beside Meeting notes or Compare means it's at work, though Draft reply has none, and Compare's badge counts checks that finished while you were elsewhere; Ask has the same spinner while a chat is being answered); and **Bookmarks** the notes you've pinned (point at one and click its × to take the bookmark away; the note stays, and ⌘Z puts it back). Knowledge health, Activity, Trash and Settings are at the foot, above the line that says how many files are indexed.

While the vault is read-only, a **Read-only** pill at the end of that line opens [Settings › Vault](app:settings/vault). If the tasks can't be loaded, Today and Tasks show a warning sign instead of a count.

`⌥⌘1` to `⌥⌘9`, then `⌥⌘0`, go to Today, Inbox, Tasks, Projects, Changes, Search, Ask, Notes, Wiki and Sources.

Messages at the bottom go after a few seconds, except an error, which stays until you close it with its ×. If a screen fails, it offers **Try again**, **Go to Today** and **Copy error details**.

## Jump anywhere with ⌘K
Press `⌘K` from any screen to find a note, go to a screen or run a command. Type a few letters of a title or a word in a note and press `↩`. `⌘↩` opens the full [Search](app:search) page for what you typed. `⌘[` and `⌘]` go back and forward through the places you've been.

## Pick where to go next
To capture and plan, start on [Today](app:today) and press `⌃⌥Space` from any app to capture a task or thought. To read and write notes, go to [Notes](app:notes) and press `⌘N` for a new one. To ask questions of your vault, open [Ask](app:ask).
